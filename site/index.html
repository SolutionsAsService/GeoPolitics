What is not drawn, and why

The 76 loose threads under the map are records that could not be drawn: 86 of the repository's 179 records name their members only in words ("27 European countries"); Wikidata filled 14 where its count agreed and refused 6 where it did not. 28 records were the same organisation listed twice and are drawn once. 17 member names are territories, regions or sentences.

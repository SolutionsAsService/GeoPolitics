# Critic: Provocateur (Shklovsky), R0

- Anchor used: Shklovsky, Art as Technique: habit devours objects; estrangement makes the stone stony by impeding perception.
- Observed: README.md lists name/type/members/purpose (a habitual catalogue). Evidence: brief is "a dynamic world map"; 90 of 191 `members` are prose ("27 European countries"); no art or games data. Artifact: `US/military/army/asoc/special_forces/oda/oda_example.json` ("Member 1", 12-man team) six folders deep beside `TaxHavens.json`, `Religon/Rastafari.json`.
- Findings:
  - F1 [premise] A coloured clickable world map is the most automatized image of geopolitics: recognition, not seeing.
  - F2 [defect] The strangest material is buried: an invented squad, a faith and tax rates filed as peers of NATO. That incongruous adjacency is the estranging device; "woven together" flattens it.
  - F3 [claim] The 90 prose memberships refuse the map; show them as the blur they are, not silently normalised.
  - F4 [premise] Promising art and games without data is the habitual presented as the new.
- Verdict: OBJECT
- Requested change: F1 demote the map to one view; lead with a device that impedes (a country seen only through its memberships). F2 make incongruous juxtapositions the entry. F3 render unresolved members as unresolved. F4 drop art/games or mark them absent.
